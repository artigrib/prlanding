SO-101 simulation — printedrobotics.cz
=====================================

This folder bundles third-party components. All are used under their licences;
copies of the licence texts are included next to each component.

1) SO-101 robot model (model/)
   Source:  MuJoCo Menagerie, robotstudio_so101
            https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/robotstudio_so101
   Origin:  The Robot Studio SO-ARM100 / SO101 (https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100)
   Licence: Apache License 2.0 (model/LICENSE)
   Changes made by printedrobotics.cz:
     - STL meshes simplified (≈ 430k -> ≈ 120k triangles) and converted to MuJoCo .msh;
       mesh files renamed from .stl to .msh in so101.xml accordingly.
     - Unused mesh asset moving_jaw_so101_gripper_v1 removed from so101.xml.
     - scene.xml is new (table, cubes, bowls, ball, goal, home keyframe); it replaces Menagerie's scene.xml.
     - The wrist camera mount is kept in physics; it is drawn only while the wrist camera view is on.
     - model/visual/so101_visual.glb (visual only, not used for physics): the original, non-simplified
       Menagerie STL files (robotstudio_so101/assets/*.stl, 14 visual meshes, 325 020 triangles) converted
       with sim/so101/tools/stl-to-glb.mjs: vertices welded, positions only (normals are computed in the
       browser), meshopt reordering, 14-bit position quantization (KHR_mesh_quantization) and
       EXT_meshopt_compression. Geometry is otherwise unchanged; coordinates stay in the STL frame.

2) MuJoCo WebAssembly bindings (vendor/mujoco/)
   Source:  npm package @mujoco/mujoco 3.17.0, Google DeepMind
            https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco
   Licence: Apache License 2.0 (vendor/mujoco/LICENSE)
   mujoco.js unmodified. Changes made by printedrobotics.cz to mujoco.wasm: debug and name custom
     sections removed (wabt wasm-strip, reproducible with tools/wasm/vendor-mujoco.mjs); code, data
     and exports are byte-identical to the npm build.

3) three.js (the site's shared ../../vendor/three/, loaded through the pages' importmap)
   Source:  npm package three 0.186.1 (build/three.module.js + three.core.js, examples/jsm/controls/OrbitControls.js,
            examples/jsm/loaders/GLTFLoader.js, examples/jsm/utils/BufferGeometryUtils.js and their imports)
   Licence: MIT (vendor/three/LICENSE)
   Unmodified; minified by tools/vendor-three.mjs (vendor/three/NOTICE.txt).

4) Feetech servo control tables and setup procedure (lib/feetech.js, lib/so101-setup.js,
   lib/sts3215-emu.js, used by the setup wizard in nastaveni/)
   Source:  LeRobot v0.6.1, Hugging Face — https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot/tree/v0.6.1
            src/lerobot/motors/feetech/tables.py   (STS/SMS control table addresses and sizes,
                                                    sign-magnitude bits, baud-rate codes,
                                                    STS3215 model number and resolution)
            src/lerobot/motors/feetech/feetech.py  (find single motor, configure_motors,
                                                    torque enable/disable with Lock, half-turn homing)
            src/lerobot/motors/motors_bus.py       (setup_motor, reset_calibration,
                                                    set_half_turn_homings, record_ranges_of_motion,
                                                    _normalize/_unnormalize)
            src/lerobot/robots/so_follower/        (motor IDs, setup order, calibration flow,
                                                    P/I/D and gripper torque settings)
   Copyright 2024 The HuggingFace Inc. team.
   Licence: Apache License 2.0 (same text as model/LICENSE; http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)
   Changes made by printedrobotics.cz: the register table and the listed procedures were re-implemented
     in JavaScript (subset of registers; Czech messages); nothing is copied verbatim beyond register
     names, addresses and constants.
   The packet format (header, length, checksum, instruction codes, error bits, sync read/write layout)
   follows Feetech's scservo_sdk 1.0.0 (PyPI feetech-servo-sdk, The Unlicense), which LeRobot uses.
   The STS3215 behaviour in lib/sts3215-emu.js (factory values, thermal and voltage behaviour) is an
   approximation written by printedrobotics.cz, not Feetech's firmware.

            https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer, Copyright (C) 2016-2026 Arseny Kapoulkine
   Licence: MIT (vendor/three/NOTICE.txt)
   Unmodified; minified.

6) Objects of missions 06–10 (objects/, src/objects/scene.js)
   "Objekty: NVIDIA SimReady (CC BY 4.0)": M8 nut and bolt, apple, banana, cupcake, plastic spoon and fork,
   cleaning sponge, Phillips PH1 screwdriver and glass vial from nvidia/simready-assets by NVIDIA, licensed
   CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the mug in mission 08 is "Mug" from Google
   Scanned Objects by Google Research, CC BY 4.0. Per-object attribution and sources: objects/LICENSES.md;
   conversion and changes: objects/NOTICE.txt.
   Further changes made by printedrobotics.cz for the missions (src/objects/scene.js):
     - M8 nut and bolt shown 2.5x larger and the sponge at 80 % (collision, mass and visual scaled together),
       so the SO-101's printed jaws can hold them; the mission text says so.
     - Collision of the banana, spoon and fork replaced by chains of convex slices along the object
       (the single convex hull is a filled shape the jaws cannot close on); mass and inertia unchanged.
     - dishware_bowl_a01: authored mass of exactly 1 kg (placeholder) set to 0.3 kg, inertia scaled.
     - Bins, plate, tray, screwdriver holder and vial rack are new, simple geometry by printedrobotics.cz.
