SO-101 simulation — object library (sim/so101/objects/, objects.html, src/objects/)
==================================================================================

1) Objects (<id>/<id>.glb, <id>/<id>.xml)
   Licences and the required attribution for every object are listed in LICENSES.md (generated from
   manifest.json by tools/assets/manifest.py). All objects are converted copies: the visual mesh is decimated
   and compressed, textures are resized/re-encoded as WebP, collision shapes are convex hulls with explicit
   mass and inertia. Conversion: tools/assets/ (see docs/objects.md).

2) three.js loaders used by the demo page (vendor/three/loaders/GLTFLoader.js,
   vendor/three/utils/BufferGeometryUtils.js, vendor/three/libs/meshopt_decoder.module.js)
   Source:  npm package three 0.160.0 (examples/jsm/), MIT (vendor/three/LICENSE);
            the meshopt decoder is part of meshoptimizer by Arseny Kapoulkine, MIT.
   Unmodified.
