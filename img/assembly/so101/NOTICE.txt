SO-101 assembly step images (img/assembly/so101/) — printedrobotics.cz
=======================================================================

Rendered by printedrobotics.cz with tools/render-kit/so101-steps.mjs (three.js, headless Chromium).
No frames from third-party videos or photos are used.

Geometry: SO-101 robot model from MuJoCo Menagerie, robotstudio_so101
          https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/robotstudio_so101
          Origin: The Robot Studio SO-ARM100 / SO101 (https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100)
          Licence: Apache License 2.0 (copy: sim/so101/model/LICENSE)
          The meshes are the ones bundled in sim/so101/model/visual/so101_visual.glb (see sim/so101/NOTICE.txt),
          placed with the body / geom poses of sim/so101/model/so101.xml, all joints at 0.

Changes / additions by printedrobotics.cz: colours, camera, exploded "ghost" positions and arrows; the horns
of the STS3215 servo are separated from its body for highlighting. Screws (M2×6, M3×6 as named in the
official LeRobot guide), the driver board and the cable are schematic 2D symbols drawn by printedrobotics.cz;
their positions are indicative, not exact hole positions.
