PR-Bench: how AI models drive a robot

We give language models the same tasks our visitors solve in the SO-101 simulator: put the cube in the bowl, build a tower, sort parts, wave. The model drives the simulated arm only through tools (joints, gripper position, scene state), the same ones behind the Connect AI button. Success is judged by the simulator's mission check, not by a person.

02 — Methodology

How we measure

  • Same conditions for everyone: the same system prompt, the same tools, temperature 0, the same step, time and token limits. No tuning for a particular model.
  • 12 tasks: missions 01–10 of the SO-101 simulator and two motion tasks (wave, draw a square), each three times with a different layout.
  • Score 0–100: success is worth 85 points, partial progress up to 15, minus 5 points for each refused unsafe motion (at most 20). Cost and steps are reported separately.
  • Hidden set: layouts derived from a secret that lives only on our server, so models cannot learn the tasks by heart.
  • Images only for multimodal models (a schematic view of the scene); text-only models work from numbers alone and are ranked separately.

Full methodology: bench/SPEC.md.
