Your build

How to submit your build

Built an SO-101 with our guide? We’d be glad to add your build (with your consent, under your name or nickname). Send us:

  • the date and rough time spent for each stage,
  • what went wrong and how you solved it,
  • a few of your own photos (that you’re happy for us to publish),
  • your LeRobot version and servo variant (7.4 V or 12 V).

Write to us through the form on the SO-101 page, in the Note field; we’ll reply with where to send the photos.
