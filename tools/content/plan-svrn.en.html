    02 — Part check

    Watch the controller, servo and LiPo

    Four N10 motors need the Malenki HV. The board has no BEC, so the servo gets the full battery voltage. And the battery must be 2S with the right plug. Mistakes show up only on the first drive, or as smoke.

            06 — Disclaimer

            We are not the seller

            • You buy the parts directly from the suppliers. The seller is always the shop: you pay it, and it owes you the warranty and the right of withdrawal. We don’t order, don’t pay and can’t see your orders.
            • Prices and stock change every week. Each line shows the date it was checked and the source status (verified, carried, unverified, estimate). Check the price at the shop before you order.
            • The landed cost is a range: goods incl. VAT (FLS Battlebots is not a VAT payer, its price is final) and our shipping estimate, verified for FLS. The exchange rate is a fixed assumption of CZK/€.
            • We summarise listing reviews in our own words, with a date; we quote nothing from them.
            • No link is an affiliate link. If that ever changes, we’ll say so right next to the link.
