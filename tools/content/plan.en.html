02 — Servo variant

Watch the variant

STS3215 servos look the same but differ in voltage and gear ratio. A wrong variant only shows during assembly: the leader is stiff or sags, and a 12 V supply destroys a 7.4 V servo. The gear ratio is only on the label and the listing, and listings can be wrong.

          06 — Disclaimer

          We are not the seller

          • You buy the parts directly from the suppliers. The seller is always the shop: you pay it, and it owes you the warranty and the right of withdrawal. We don’t order, don’t pay and can’t see your orders.
          • Prices and stock change every week. Each line shows the date it was checked and the source status (verified, carried, unverified, estimate). Check the price at the shop before you order.
          • The landed cost is a range: goods incl. VAT, a possible switch to Czech 21 % VAT at EU shops (up to +1.7 %) and our shipping estimate. The exchange rate is a fixed assumption of CZK/€.
          • Always check the servo variant on the listing and on the label. We can’t vouch for a shop’s listing, but we’ll gladly fix an error in the plan.
          • No link is an affiliate link. If that ever changes, we’ll say so right next to the link.
