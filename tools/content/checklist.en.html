SO-101: build checklist

Based on LeRobot (huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/so101) and SO-ARM100 (github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100), as of 29 Oct 2026. Screws: M2×6 and M3×6 only.

0 · Before you build
  • Phillips #0 and #1 screwdrivers, clamps, a screw tray
  • Follower: 6× 1/345 (C001). Leader: ID 1 and 3 = 1/191 (C044), ID 2 = 1/345 (C001), ID 4–6 = 1/147 (C046)
  • Servos labelled (L/F + joint number)
  • 5 V supply for 7.4 V servos · 12 V (5 A+) only for 12 V follower servos
1 · Printing
  • PLA+, 15% infill, 0.4 nozzle / 0.2 mm layers (or 0.6 / 0.4)
  • Supports yes, but not in horizontal screw holes
  • Gauge 0 fits the servo
  • Supports removed, holes and pockets clean
2 · Servo IDs (before assembly)
  • LeRobot installed, ports of both boards written down
  • Waveshare: both jumpers on channel B
  • Only one servo on the board at a time, IDs in order 6 → 1
  • A 3-pin cable plugged into every servo
3 · Mechanics (for each arm)
  • Joint 1 base · Joint 2 shoulder · Joint 3 elbow
  • Joint 4: holder first, then servo · Joint 5: one horn only
  • Gripper (F) / handle and trigger (L)
  • Top horn M3×6, bottom horn without a screw, horn fully on the splines
  • Cables don’t get pinched as joints move · screws only to resistance
4 · Electronics
  • Chain board → ID 1 → … → ID 6, connectors clicked in
  • Right supply on the right arm, supply within reach
5 · First motion
  • Arms clamped, nothing within reach
  • Calibration: joints to the middle, Enter, each joint through its full range (F and L)
  • Teleoperation: the follower copies all 6 joints
  • If anything goes wrong: pull the servo supply
