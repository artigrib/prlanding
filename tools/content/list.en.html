Task 3 · An algorithm for mission 03 “Sort the colours” In pairs, write the steps the arm follows to put the orange cube in the orange bowl and the blue cube in the blue bowl. Then test them in the simulation. 1.2.3.4.5.6.7.8. Did the steps work first time? yes / no. What did you fix?
Task 4 · Learning from demonstration In the Learning from demonstration panel, record your solution to mission 01 (Record) and play it back (Play). The recording always replays the same joint angles. What would happen if the cube were 5 cm away from where it was? Why?

For teachers: answer guide
  • Task 2: base rotation turns the whole arm left and right; shoulder and elbow raise and extend the arm; wrist pitch points the gripper down; wrist roll turns the jaws. The angles depend on where the cube is; any pose from which the cube can be grasped is correct.
  • Task 2: smart move changes several joints at once so the gripper reaches the chosen point (inverse kinematics). Joint by joint, the student has to “calculate” it themselves.
  • Task 3: a good algorithm opens the gripper before going down to the cube, closes it, lifts, moves above the bowl, releases, and repeats for the second cube. An excellent one handles a mistake (the cube dropped → grasp again).
  • Task 4: the arm would go down where the cube was during recording and miss it. That is why real robots learn from many demonstrations with the object in different places.
