ROBOTO ORIGIN

A humanoid robot by RoboParty: 23 joints and a machined-aluminium frame. According to its authors the whole development is open source: mechanics, electronics, training and deployment. In the simulation it walks with the policy its authors trained and released.

A showcase, not a product: we do not sell parts or kits for this robot. We are not affiliated with RoboParty.

The physics is approximate and the real robot behaves differently. Walking is driven by the policy RoboParty trained and published; we only run it in the browser. A computer with a graphics card is recommended.

Performance: this device has weaker graphics, so shadows are off.

Mode

Keys: W/S forward and back · A/D turn · Q/E sidestep · a gamepad works too

About the simulation and licences

Model, meshes and walking policy: RoboParty roboto_origin (robot description CERN-OHL-W-2.0, policy and software GPL-3.0). We simplified the meshes for the browser; everything else is unchanged – licences, changes and exact version. Physics by MuJoCo; the policy runs on ONNX Runtime Web. We are not affiliated with RoboParty; ROBOTO ORIGIN and RoboParty are their marks. The simulation is simplified; the real robot behaves differently.
